FAQ

Pricing questions

What counts as a prospected contact?

A person A-Gent researches and drafts outreach for in your workspace. Monthly allowance details are confirmed at checkout.

Does A-Gent ever send without my approval?

No. Every outbound message requires approval by a person on your team in the Review Queue before it is sent.

What happens when my trial ends?

You can choose Starter or Growth at any point during the 15-day trial. If you cancel during the trial you are not charged. After the trial, the workspace stays on the plan you selected at signup.

Can I cancel?

Yes. You can cancel during the trial with no charge. After you become a paying customer, you can cancel at the end of the current billing period from Mission Control or by contacting us.

Do you offer annual billing?

Plans are billed monthly today. Annual billing may be added later.
